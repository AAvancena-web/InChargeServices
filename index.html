Questions

Common questions, answered

Do I need an NDIS plan to use In Charge Services?
No. We support NDIS participants (agency, plan-managed and self-managed) as well as private clients who simply want reliable help at home. Tell us your situation and we'll explain your options.
How quickly can supports start?
Most enquiries get a call back within one business day. After a free meet & greet, supports commonly start within the same week depending on the services and hours you need.
Which areas of Adelaide do you cover?
We're based at Level 1, 89 Pirie Street in the Adelaide CBD and support participants right across the Adelaide metropolitan area. Not sure if we reach your suburb? Give us a call.
Can I choose or change my support worker?
Absolutely. You meet your support worker before supports begin, and if the match isn't right we'll find someone who is, at no cost to you.
How much does it cost?
Our NDIS services are charged in line with the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. For private and domestic work we'll give you a clear quote before anything starts.
Do you accept referrals from support coordinators?
Yes, referrals are always welcome from support coordinators, plan managers, allied health professionals and families. Send the details through our referral page or call us directly.
